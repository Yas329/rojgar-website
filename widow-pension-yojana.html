Widow Pension Yojana 2026

Widow Pension Yojana is a government welfare scheme launched to provide monthly financial assistance and social security support to widowed women across India.

The scheme helps economically weaker widows by offering pension benefits for daily expenses, healthcare and financial stability.

Widow Pension Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Widow Pension Yojana 2026
Launched By Government of India / State Governments
Beneficiaries Widowed Women
Main Benefit Monthly Pension Assistance
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Portal NSAP / State Portals

Benefits of Widow Pension Yojana

  • Monthly financial pension support
  • Social security assistance for widows
  • Support for economically weaker women
  • Direct bank transfer of pension amount
  • Financial help for daily expenses
  • Improved social welfare support
  • Government-backed pension benefits

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Only widowed women can apply
  • Applicant should belong to eligible income category
  • Valid Aadhaar and bank account required
  • Age criteria may vary by state government

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Husband Death Certificate
  • Bank Account Passbook
  • Income Certificate
  • Residence Certificate
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply Online for Widow Pension Yojana?

  1. Visit the official pension portal of your state
  2. Open Widow Pension Scheme application form
  3. Fill personal and pension details carefully
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Submit application form online
  6. Verification process will be completed by authorities
  7. Approved beneficiaries receive pension in bank account
