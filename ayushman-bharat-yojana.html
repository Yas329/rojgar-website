Ayushman Bharat Yojana 2026

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is one of the largest health insurance schemes launched by the Government of India. Under this scheme, eligible families receive free health insurance coverage up to ₹5 lakh per year for medical treatment at empanelled hospitals across India.

Ayushman Bharat Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
Launched By Government of India
Department National Health Authority (NHA)
Benefit Amount ₹5 Lakh Health Insurance
Beneficiaries Eligible Indian Families
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website pmjay.gov.in

Benefits of Ayushman Bharat Yojana

  • Free medical treatment up to ₹5 lakh per family.
  • Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals.
  • Covers surgeries, hospitalization and medicines.
  • Available across government and private hospitals.
  • Provides financial protection during medical emergencies.

Ayushman Bharat Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen.
  • Families listed in SECC 2011 database are eligible.
  • Economically weaker sections can apply.
  • Rural and urban poor families are covered under the scheme.
  • Eligibility may vary according to government guidelines.

Documents Required for Ayushman Card

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ration Card
  • Mobile Number
  • Residence Certificate
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Family Identification Documents

How to Apply Online for Ayushman Bharat Yojana?

  1. Visit the official PM-JAY portal.
  2. Check eligibility using mobile number or ration card.
  3. Complete eKYC verification process.
  4. Upload required documents.
  5. Submit the application form.
  6. Download Ayushman Card after approval.

How to Download Ayushman Card?

  1. Visit official Ayushman Bharat website.
  2. Login using registered mobile number.
  3. Verify OTP.
  4. Select beneficiary details.
  5. Download Ayushman Card PDF.

How to Check Ayushman Bharat Hospital List?

Beneficiaries can search empanelled hospitals online through the official PM-JAY portal by selecting state, district and hospital type.

  • Government Hospitals
  • Private Hospitals
  • Multi-speciality Hospitals
  • Emergency Healthcare Centers

Ayushman Bharat Yojana FAQs

What is Ayushman Bharat Yojana?

Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is a government health insurance scheme providing up to ₹5 lakh free treatment coverage.

Who can apply for Ayushman Bharat?

Eligible families listed under SECC database and economically weaker sections can apply.

How to download Ayushman Card?

Applicants can download Ayushman Card online through the official PM-JAY portal.

Is private hospital treatment covered?

Yes, treatment is available at empanelled government and private hospitals.
