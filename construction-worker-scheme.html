Construction Worker Scheme 2026

Construction Worker Scheme is a government welfare initiative designed to provide financial assistance, insurance coverage and social security benefits to construction workers and labourers across India.

The scheme supports registered construction workers through healthcare, education assistance, pensions, housing support and accident insurance benefits.

Construction Worker Scheme Overview

Scheme Name Construction Worker Scheme 2026
Launched By Government of India / State Labour Departments
Beneficiaries Construction Workers and Labourers
Main Benefit Worker Welfare and Financial Assistance
Registration Mode Online / Offline
Official Portal eShram Portal

Benefits of Construction Worker Scheme

  • Accident insurance coverage for workers
  • Financial assistance and welfare support
  • Healthcare and medical benefits
  • Scholarship assistance for children
  • Maternity and pension benefits
  • Housing and social welfare support
  • Support during emergencies and accidents

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Construction workers and labourers can apply
  • Applicant should work in construction related activities
  • Valid Aadhaar linked mobile number required
  • Age should generally be between 18 to 60 years

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Mobile Number
  • Bank Passbook
  • Residence Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Worker or Labour Proof
  • Identity Proof
  • Employment Details

How to Apply Online for Construction Worker Scheme?

  1. Visit the official labour or eShram portal
  2. Complete worker registration process
  3. Enter Aadhaar and personal details
  4. Fill occupation and employment information
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Submit registration form online
  7. Download labour or worker card after approval
