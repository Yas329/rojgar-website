SSC Scheme of Examination 2026

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) Scheme of Examination defines the complete exam structure, syllabus, marking system, and selection process for all SSC exams such as CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD Constable, JE, CPO and others.

Official SSC Scheme

SSC Exams Covered Under Scheme

  • SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level)
  • SSC CHSL (Combined Higher Secondary Level)
  • SSC MTS (Multi Tasking Staff)
  • SSC GD Constable
  • SSC JE (Junior Engineer)
  • SSC CPO (Sub Inspector)
  • SSC Stenographer
  • Selection Post Exams

SSC Exam Pattern Overview

Exam Mode Sections Marks
SSC CGL CBT Reasoning, Maths, English, GK 200+
SSC CHSL CBT + Descriptive English, Maths, Reasoning 200
SSC MTS CBT General Awareness, Reasoning, Maths Variable
SSC GD CBT + PET/PST GK, Reasoning, Maths 100

SSC Selection Process

  1. Online Application
  2. Computer Based Test (CBT)
  3. Skill Test / Physical Test (if required)
  4. Document Verification
  5. Final Merit List

Important Points for SSC Exams

  • Negative marking is applicable in most SSC exams
  • Exam pattern may change based on official notification
  • Practice previous year question papers
  • Check official SSC website regularly
  • Time management is very important
