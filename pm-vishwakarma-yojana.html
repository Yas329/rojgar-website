PM Vishwakarma Yojana 2026

PM Vishwakarma Yojana is a central government scheme launched to support traditional artisans and craftsmen by providing financial assistance, skill training, toolkit incentives and business support.

The scheme helps workers such as carpenters, blacksmiths, tailors, potters, barbers, goldsmiths and other traditional artisans improve their income and business opportunities.

PM Vishwakarma Yojana Overview

Scheme Name PM Vishwakarma Yojana 2026
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries Traditional Artisans and Craftsmen
Main Benefit Loan, Training and Toolkit Support
Loan Benefit Collateral Free Business Loan
Application Mode Online
Official Website pmvishwakarma.gov.in

Benefits of PM Vishwakarma Yojana

  • Collateral free business loan support
  • Skill development and training programs
  • Toolkit incentive for artisans
  • Digital transaction incentives
  • Marketing and branding support
  • Financial support for self-employment
  • Improved business opportunities for craftsmen

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Traditional artisans and craftsmen are eligible
  • Applicant should work in recognized traditional trades
  • Minimum age should be 18 years
  • Valid Aadhaar linked mobile number required

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Mobile Number
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Residence Proof
  • Occupation or Trade Proof
  • Identity Proof

How to Apply Online for PM Vishwakarma Yojana?

  1. Visit the official PM Vishwakarma portal
  2. Complete online registration process
  3. Fill application form carefully
  4. Select trade or occupation category
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Verify Aadhaar and mobile details
  7. Submit application form online
  8. Track application status online
