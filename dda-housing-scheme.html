DDA Housing Scheme 2026

DDA Housing Scheme is an affordable housing initiative launched by the Delhi Development Authority (DDA) to provide flats and housing opportunities to citizens in Delhi.

The scheme offers flats for different income groups including EWS, LIG, MIG and HIG categories through online registration and lottery systems.

DDA Housing Scheme Overview

Scheme Name DDA Housing Scheme 2026
Launched By Delhi Development Authority
Beneficiaries Delhi Residents and Eligible Citizens
Main Benefit Affordable Flats and Housing
Flat Categories EWS, LIG, MIG and HIG
Application Mode Online
Official Website dda.gov.in

Benefits of DDA Housing Scheme

  • Affordable housing opportunities in Delhi
  • Flats available for multiple income groups
  • Transparent online application and lottery system
  • Government-authorized residential properties
  • Easy online registration process
  • Housing support for economically weaker sections
  • Modern infrastructure and residential facilities

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Applicant should be at least 18 years old
  • Valid identity and address proof required
  • Applicant should meet category income criteria
  • Bank account details should be available

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Residence Proof
  • Income Certificate
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Bank Account Details
  • Mobile Number
  • Category Certificate (if applicable)

How to Apply Online for DDA Housing Scheme?

  1. Visit the official DDA portal
  2. Complete online registration process
  3. Select desired flat category
  4. Fill application form carefully
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Pay registration fees online
  7. Submit application form
  8. Check lottery and allotment results online
