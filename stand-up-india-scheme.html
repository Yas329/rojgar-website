Stand Up India Scheme 2026

Stand Up India Scheme is a government initiative launched to support entrepreneurship among women and SC/ST communities by providing bank loans for setting up new businesses.

The scheme helps eligible applicants start enterprises in manufacturing, trading and service sectors through financial assistance and business support.

Official Website

Stand Up India Scheme Overview

Scheme Name Stand Up India Scheme 2026
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries Women and SC/ST Entrepreneurs
Main Benefit Business Loan Assistance
Loan Amount ₹10 Lakh to ₹1 Crore
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website standupmitra.in

Benefits of Stand Up India Scheme

  • Business loan support for new enterprises
  • Financial assistance for women entrepreneurs
  • Support for SC/ST business owners
  • Encourages self-employment opportunities
  • Easy access to bank financing
  • Support for manufacturing and service businesses
  • Promotes entrepreneurship development in India

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Women entrepreneurs are eligible
  • SC/ST category applicants can apply
  • Age should generally be above 18 years
  • Business should be a new enterprise
  • Applicant should not have defaulted on bank loans

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Caste Certificate (for SC/ST applicants)
  • Business Plan
  • Bank Account Details
  • Residence Proof
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number and Email ID

How to Apply Online for Stand Up India Scheme?

  1. Visit the official Stand Up India portal
  2. Register using mobile number and email
  3. Fill business and personal details
  4. Select business category and loan amount
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Submit application form online
  7. Bank verification and approval process will begin
