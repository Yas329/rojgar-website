Startup India Scheme 2026

Startup India Scheme is a flagship initiative launched by the Government of India to support startups, entrepreneurs and innovation across the country.

The scheme provides tax benefits, startup recognition, easier compliance, funding support, incubation assistance and business development opportunities for eligible startups.

Startup India Scheme Overview

Scheme Name Startup India Scheme 2026
Launched By Government of India
Department DPIIT
Beneficiaries Startups and Entrepreneurs
Main Benefit Startup Recognition and Business Support
Application Mode Online
Official Website startupindia.gov.in

Benefits of Startup India Scheme

  • Tax exemption benefits for eligible startups
  • Easy startup registration process
  • Government funding and support programs
  • Access to incubators and mentorship
  • Intellectual property and patent support
  • Networking and investment opportunities
  • Reduced compliance burden for startups

Eligibility Criteria

  • Startup must be registered in India
  • Entity age should generally be within government limits
  • Business should focus on innovation or improvement
  • Annual turnover should be within prescribed limit
  • Startup must not be formed by splitting existing business

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Business Registration Certificate
  • Company Incorporation Documents
  • Bank Account Details
  • Startup Pitch or Business Plan
  • Mobile Number and Email ID
  • Address Proof

How to Apply Online for Startup India Scheme?

  1. Visit the official Startup India portal
  2. Create account using mobile number and email
  3. Register startup details online
  4. Upload required business documents
  5. Apply for DPIIT recognition
  6. Submit application form
  7. Track application status online
