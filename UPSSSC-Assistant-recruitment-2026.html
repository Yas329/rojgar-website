Preparation Strategy

  • Understand complete syllabus and latest exam pattern before starting preparation.
  • Focus mainly on Agriculture Subject because it carries maximum weightage in the exam.
  • Make short notes for important Agriculture topics, formulas and current affairs.
  • Practice UPSSSC PET level Reasoning and General Knowledge questions daily.
  • Read daily current affairs from newspapers and monthly current affairs PDFs.
  • Solve previous year question papers to understand exam difficulty and question trends.
  • Attempt full length mock tests regularly to improve speed and accuracy.
  • Analyze mock test performance and work on weak subjects regularly.
  • Revise important topics every week to improve long term memory.
  • Practice time management during mock tests to complete paper within time limit.
  • Prepare important Agriculture schemes, soil science, horticulture and crop production topics thoroughly.
  • Improve accuracy because negative marking may affect final score.
  • Avoid studying too many books and focus on limited quality study material.
  • Maintain proper study timetable and follow it consistently every day.
  • Stay updated with official UPSSSC notifications and exam updates.

How To Fill UPSSSC Technical Assistant Online Form 2026

Step Details
Step 1 Visit the official UPSSSC website and read the official notification carefully before applying online.
Step 2 Check eligibility criteria including age limit, educational qualification and UPSSSC PET 2025 requirement.
Step 3 Keep all important documents ready such as Aadhaar Card, PET Score Card, Photograph, Signature and Educational Certificates.
Step 4 Click on the Apply Online link and complete registration using valid mobile number and email ID.
Step 5 Fill all details carefully including personal information, educational qualification and communication details.
Step 6 Upload scanned passport size photograph, signature and required documents in proper format.
Step 7 Pay the application fee through online mode such as Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking or UPI.
Step 8 Preview the application form carefully before final submission.
Step 9 Submit the application form and take a printout of final submitted form for future reference.
Important : Candidates are advised to fill the form before the last date to avoid server issues and keep all original documents ready for verification.
