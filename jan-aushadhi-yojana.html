Jan Aushadhi Yojana 2026

Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) is a government scheme launched to provide quality generic medicines at affordable prices to citizens across India.

Under this scheme, Jan Aushadhi Kendras are opened across the country to make low-cost medicines available for the public while promoting healthcare accessibility.

Jan Aushadhi Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana
Short Name PMBJP
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries All Indian Citizens
Main Benefit Affordable Generic Medicines
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website janaushadhi.gov.in

Benefits of Jan Aushadhi Yojana

  • Quality generic medicines at affordable prices
  • Reduces healthcare and medicine expenses
  • Medicines available across India through Jan Aushadhi Kendras
  • Supports healthcare accessibility for poor families
  • Promotes awareness about generic medicines
  • Employment opportunities through Jan Aushadhi stores
  • Helps improve public healthcare system

Eligibility Criteria

  • Indian citizens can purchase medicines from Jan Aushadhi Kendras
  • Individuals, pharmacists and organizations can apply to open Kendra
  • Applicant should have required shop space for Kendra setup
  • Valid pharmacy license may be required
  • Required documents should be available during registration

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Pharmacist Registration Certificate
  • Shop Ownership / Rent Agreement
  • Bank Passbook
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • GST Registration (if applicable)

How to Apply for Jan Aushadhi Kendra?

  1. Visit the official Jan Aushadhi portal
  2. Download or fill online application form
  3. Enter personal and business details carefully
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Submit application form online
  6. Wait for document verification process
  7. Receive approval for Jan Aushadhi Kendra setup
