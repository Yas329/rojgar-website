What is SSC Tentative Vacancy?

SSC Tentative Vacancy means the approximate number of posts announced by SSC before final recruitment. These numbers are collected from different government departments and may increase or decrease later.

Why SSC Tentative Vacancy is Important?

  • Helps candidates understand competition level
  • Gives idea about total job opportunities
  • Useful for preparation planning
  • Updated before final notification
  • Helps in choosing exam strategy

How SSC Updates Vacancies?

  1. Departments send requirement to SSC
  2. SSC collects data from ministries
  3. Vacancy list is published as tentative
  4. Final vacancies are released later

Important Note

  • Tentative vacancies are not final
  • Final number may increase or decrease
  • Always check official SSC website
  • Do not depend only on tentative data
