RRB ALP Preparation Strategy

Preparation Topic Strategy
Understand Syllabus Check complete RRB ALP syllabus and latest CBT 1 & CBT 2 exam pattern before preparation.
Mathematics Practice Practice Arithmetic, Algebra, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Time & Work, Simplification and DI daily.
Reasoning Preparation Focus on puzzles, coding-decoding, blood relation, syllogism and analytical reasoning questions.
General Science Study Physics, Chemistry and Biology basics from NCERT level for CBT exams.
Current Affairs Read daily current affairs, railway updates and monthly GK PDFs regularly.
Technical Subjects Prepare trade-related technical subjects thoroughly for CBT 2 technical section.
Previous Year Papers Solve previous year RRB ALP papers to understand question trends and exam difficulty.
Mock Tests Attempt full-length online mock tests regularly to improve speed and accuracy.
Time Management Practice solving questions within limited time to improve exam performance.
Short Notes Prepare short notes for formulas, important concepts and quick revision.
Revision Revise important topics weekly to improve long-term memory and accuracy.
Accuracy Improvement Avoid guesswork because negative marking can reduce overall score.
Study Material Use limited and high-quality books instead of studying too many resources.
Daily Study Plan Maintain a proper study timetable and follow it consistently every day.
Exam Updates Stay updated with official RRB notifications, admit card and exam dates.

How To Fill RRB ALP Recruitment 2026 Form

Step Details
Step 1 Visit the official RRB website and read the official notification carefully before applying online.
Step 2 Check eligibility criteria including age limit, educational qualification.
Step 3 Keep all important documents ready such as Aadhaar Card, Photograph, Signature and Educational Certificates.
Step 4 Click on the Apply Online link and complete registration using valid mobile number and email ID.
Step 5 Fill all details carefully including personal information, educational qualification and communication details.
Step 6 Upload scanned passport size photograph, signature and required documents in proper format.
Step 7 Pay the application fee through online mode such as Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking or UPI.
Step 8 Preview the application form carefully before final submission.
Step 9 Submit the application form and take a printout of final submitted form for future reference.
Important : Candidates are advised to fill the form before the last date to avoid server issues and keep all original documents ready for verification.
