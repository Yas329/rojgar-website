Kisan Credit Card Yojana 2026

Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Yojana is a government scheme launched to provide short-term loans and financial assistance to farmers for agricultural activities. Farmers can use KCC for crop production, farming equipment, irrigation, animal husbandry and other farming needs.

Under this scheme, eligible farmers can get loans at low interest rates through banks and financial institutions.

Kisan Credit Card Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Kisan Credit Card Yojana
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries Farmers
Loan Amount Up to ₹3 Lakh or more
Interest Rate Starting from 4%
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Portal myscheme.gov.in

Benefits of Kisan Credit Card Scheme

  • Low interest agricultural loans for farmers
  • Easy access to crop loans
  • Financial support for farming activities
  • Flexible repayment options
  • Insurance coverage benefits
  • Support for animal husbandry and fisheries
  • Simple and fast application process

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Applicant should be a farmer
  • Small and marginal farmers can apply
  • Tenant farmers and sharecroppers are also eligible
  • Applicant should have farming-related activities

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Residence Certificate
  • Land Documents
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Bank Account Passbook
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply Online for Kisan Credit Card?

  1. Visit the official bank or government portal
  2. Open the Kisan Credit Card application page
  3. Fill in personal and farming details
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Submit the application form
  6. Bank verification will be completed
  7. After approval, KCC card will be issued
