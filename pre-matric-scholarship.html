Pre Matric Scholarship 2026

Pre Matric Scholarship is a government scholarship scheme created to provide financial assistance to school students studying from Class 1 to Class 10.

Eligible students from SC, ST, OBC, Minority and economically weaker categories can apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) to receive scholarship benefits.

Pre Matric Scholarship Overview

Scheme Name Pre Matric Scholarship Scheme
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries School Students
Main Benefit Educational Financial Assistance
Application Mode Online
Official Website scholarships.gov.in

Benefits of Pre Matric Scholarship

  • Financial support for school education
  • Scholarship amount transferred directly to bank account
  • Helps students continue studies without financial burden
  • Supports students from economically weaker families
  • Encourages school education among children
  • Online application and transparent selection process
  • Reduces school dropout rates

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Student should study between Class 1 to Class 10
  • Applicant must belong to eligible category
  • Family income should meet scholarship limits
  • Students must study in recognized schools

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Income Certificate
  • Caste Certificate
  • School ID Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Previous Class Marksheet

How to Apply Online for Pre Matric Scholarship?

  1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal
  2. Complete student registration process
  3. Login using registered credentials
  4. Select Pre Matric Scholarship scheme
  5. Fill personal and academic details carefully
  6. Upload required documents
  7. Submit application form online
