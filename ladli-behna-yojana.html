Ladli Behna Yojana 2026

Ladli Behna Yojana is a welfare scheme launched to provide monthly financial assistance to eligible women. The scheme aims to improve the economic condition and social security of women from economically weaker families.

Under this scheme, eligible women receive direct financial support in their bank accounts every month to help with daily expenses and family support.

Ladli Behna Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Ladli Behna Yojana
Launched By State Government
Beneficiaries Women
Main Benefit Monthly Financial Assistance
Payment Mode Direct Bank Transfer
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website cmladlibahna.mp.gov.in

Benefits of Ladli Behna Yojana

  • Monthly financial support for women
  • Improves economic independence
  • Direct bank account transfer facility
  • Support for poor and middle-class families
  • Helps women manage household expenses
  • Promotes women empowerment
  • Easy registration process

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be a woman citizen of India
  • Applicant should belong to eligible families
  • Age should generally be between 21 to 60 years
  • Applicant should have Aadhaar-linked bank account
  • Women from economically weaker sections are eligible

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Samagra ID
  • Bank Passbook
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Residence Certificate
  • Income Certificate

How to Apply Online for Ladli Behna Yojana?

  1. Visit the official Ladli Behna Yojana website
  2. Open the registration or application section
  3. Fill personal and bank details carefully
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Verify Aadhaar and mobile number
  6. Submit the application form
  7. Download or print application receipt
