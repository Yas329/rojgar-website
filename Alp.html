RRB ALP 2026

About RRB

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) is a government organization under the Ministry of Railways, responsible for recruiting candidates for various posts in Indian Railways such as NTPC, Group D, JE, and ALP. Every year, RRB conducts competitive exams across India, offering secure government jobs with attractive salary, benefits, and career growth. The selection process includes CBT exams, skill tests, document verification, and medical tests, ensuring a transparent and merit-based recruitment system.

About RRB ALP

RRB ALP is a railway recruitment exam conducted by the Railway Recruitment Board to hire Assistant Loco Pilots in Indian Railways. This is a highly popular government job offering stable career growth, attractive salary, and allowances. The selection process includes CBT Stage 1, CBT Stage 2, aptitude test, document verification, and medical examination. Candidates with 10th + ITI or Diploma qualification can apply for this post.

RRB ALP Eligibility 2026 (Educational Qualification)
  • Candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th Class) or SSLC from a recognized board.
  • Must hold an ITI Certificate in relevant trades such as Fitter, Electrician, Machinist, Diesel Mechanic, or Electronics Mechanic from NCVT/SCVT or completed Act Apprenticeship training.
  • Candidates with a 3-year Diploma in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, or Automobile Engineering are also eligible.
  • A Degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, or Automobile) is also accepted in place of a diploma.
  • This eligibility ensures candidates have both academic knowledge and technical skills required for the Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) 2026 post in Indian Railways.

How to Apply Online for RRB ALP 2026

Candidates interested in RRB ALP Recruitment 2026 can apply online through the official Railway Recruitment Board portal. Follow the step-by-step process carefully to successfully submit the application form.

  • Visit the official website of RRB or use the direct apply link provided on this page.
  • Click on the “New Registration” option and complete basic registration using name, mobile number, and email ID.
  • After registration, log in using your registration ID and password.
  • Fill the online application form carefully with personal, educational, and contact details.
  • Upload required documents such as photograph, signature, and educational certificates in the prescribed format.
  • Pay the application fee online using UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking.
  • Review the complete application form before final submission to avoid mistakes.
  • Submit the form and download/print the final application confirmation for future reference.

✔ Candidates must ensure that all details are correct before submission, as incorrect information may lead to rejection of the application.
