Old Age Pension Yojana 2026

Old Age Pension Yojana is a government welfare scheme launched to provide monthly financial assistance and social security support to senior citizens and elderly people across India.

The scheme helps economically weaker senior citizens by offering regular pension benefits to support daily living expenses and improve financial stability after retirement age.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Applicant should be a senior citizen
  • Minimum age criteria may vary by state
  • Applicant should belong to eligible income category
  • Valid Aadhaar and bank account required

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Age Proof Certificate
  • Bank Account Passbook
  • Income Certificate
  • Residence Certificate
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number

How to Apply Online for Old Age Pension Yojana?

  1. Visit the official pension scheme portal
  2. Open Old Age Pension application form
  3. Fill personal and pension details carefully
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Submit application form online
  6. Verification process will be completed by authorities
  7. Approved beneficiaries receive pension in bank account
