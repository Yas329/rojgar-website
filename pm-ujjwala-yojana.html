PM Ujjwala Yojana 2026

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) is a flagship welfare scheme launched by the Government of India to provide free LPG gas connections to women from economically weaker families.

The scheme aims to reduce smoke pollution from traditional cooking methods and improve the health and living conditions of women across rural and urban India.

PM Ujjwala Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana
Short Name PMUY
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries Women from Poor Families
Main Benefit Free LPG Gas Connection
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website www.pmuy.gov.in

Benefits of PM Ujjwala Yojana

  • Free LPG gas connection for eligible women
  • Cleaner and smoke-free cooking environment
  • Improves women's health and safety
  • Reduces indoor air pollution
  • Financial support for LPG setup
  • Promotes clean fuel usage in rural areas
  • Easy application and verification process

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be a woman citizen of India
  • Age should be 18 years or above
  • Applicant should belong to eligible low-income families
  • No existing LPG connection in the household
  • Applicant should have Aadhaar-linked bank account

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Ration Card
  • Residence Proof
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • BPL Certificate (if required)

How to Apply Online for PM Ujjwala Yojana?

  1. Visit the official PMUY website
  2. Select your LPG distributor company
  3. Download or fill application form online
  4. Enter personal and address details carefully
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Submit the application form
  7. Wait for verification and approval process
