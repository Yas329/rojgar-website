National Scholarship Scheme 2026

National Scholarship Scheme is a government initiative to provide financial assistance to eligible students for higher education and school studies across India.

Students from economically weaker sections can apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) to receive scholarship benefits directly in their bank accounts.

National Scholarship Scheme Overview

Scheme Name National Scholarship Scheme
Portal Name National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries School & College Students
Main Benefit Financial Assistance for Education
Application Mode Online
Official Website scholarships.gov.in

Benefits of National Scholarship Scheme

  • Financial support for school and college education
  • Direct scholarship transfer to bank accounts
  • Helps economically weaker students continue studies
  • Supports technical and professional courses
  • Online application and verification system
  • Reduces financial burden on families
  • Encourages higher education in India

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Student should be studying in recognized institutions
  • Applicant must meet income criteria
  • Minimum academic performance may be required
  • Students should have valid bank account details

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Income Certificate
  • Previous Class Marksheet
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Institution Verification Documents

How to Apply Online for National Scholarship Scheme?

  1. Visit the National Scholarship Portal
  2. Complete new student registration
  3. Login using application credentials
  4. Select scholarship category and scheme
  5. Fill personal and academic details carefully
  6. Upload required documents
  7. Submit application form for verification
