SSC CGL Exam Pattern 2026

  • Tier 1: Objective Type Computer Based Test

SSC CGL Syllabus 2026

SSC CGL syllabus includes General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude, English Language, General Awareness and Computer Knowledge subjects.

What is SSC CGL?

SSC CGL is a national level government recruitment examination conducted by Staff Selection Commission for graduate candidates.

Candidates having graduation degree from recognized university can apply.

What is the age limit for SSC CGL?

Candidates between 18 to 32 years can apply depending on post.

What is SSC CGL salary?

SSC CGL salary ranges between ₹25,500 to ₹81,100 depending on post and pay level.
