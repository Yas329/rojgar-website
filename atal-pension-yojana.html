Atal Pension Yojana 2026

Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a government-backed pension scheme launched to provide financial security and guaranteed monthly pension benefits to citizens after retirement.

The scheme mainly targets workers from the unorganized sector and helps individuals build a stable retirement income through regular contributions.

Atal Pension Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Atal Pension Yojana 2026
Launched By Government of India
Scheme Type Pension Scheme
Beneficiaries Indian Citizens
Main Benefit Guaranteed Monthly Pension
Application Mode Online / Bank Registration
Official Portal NPS / APY Portal

Benefits of Atal Pension Yojana

  • Guaranteed monthly pension after retirement
  • Financial security during old age
  • Low monthly contribution options
  • Government-supported pension scheme
  • Easy registration through banks
  • Support for unorganized sector workers
  • Pension benefits for spouse in some cases

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Age should be between 18 to 40 years
  • Applicant should have a savings bank account
  • Valid Aadhaar and mobile number required
  • Regular contribution is mandatory till retirement age

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Account Passbook
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Identity Proof
  • Residence Proof

How to Apply Online for Atal Pension Yojana?

  1. Visit your nearest bank branch or official portal
  2. Fill Atal Pension Yojana registration form
  3. Provide Aadhaar and bank account details
  4. Select desired monthly pension amount
  5. Choose contribution frequency
  6. Submit application form
  7. Monthly contribution will auto-debit from bank account
