PM Matru Vandana Yojana 2026

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) is a maternity benefit scheme launched by the Government of India to provide financial assistance to pregnant women and lactating mothers.

The scheme helps women receive proper nutrition, healthcare and financial support during pregnancy and after childbirth.

Official Website

PM Matru Vandana Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana
Short Name PMMVY
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries Pregnant Women and Lactating Mothers
Main Benefit Financial Assistance During Pregnancy
Financial Benefit Up to ₹5000 Assistance
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website pmmvy.wcd.gov.in

Benefits of PM Matru Vandana Yojana

  • Financial assistance during pregnancy
  • Support for proper nutrition and healthcare
  • Encourages institutional delivery and medical care
  • Improves health of mother and child
  • Direct benefit transfer into bank account
  • Helps reduce maternal and infant health risks
  • Supports economically weaker families

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Pregnant women and lactating mothers are eligible
  • Applicant should meet scheme guidelines
  • Required documents must be available
  • Bank account should be linked with Aadhaar

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Mother and Child Protection Card
  • Pregnancy Registration Documents
  • Mobile Number
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Residence Proof
  • Identity Proof

How to Apply Online for PM Matru Vandana Yojana?

  1. Visit the official PMMVY portal
  2. Register using required details
  3. Fill maternity benefit application form
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Verify all information carefully
  6. Submit application form online
  7. Track application status online
