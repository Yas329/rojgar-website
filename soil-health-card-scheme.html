Soil Health Card Scheme 2026

Soil Health Card Scheme is a government initiative launched to help farmers improve soil fertility and crop productivity through scientific soil testing.

Under this scheme, farmers receive a Soil Health Card containing information about soil nutrients, fertilizer recommendations and methods to improve soil quality.

Soil Health Card Scheme Overview

Scheme Name Soil Health Card Scheme
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries Farmers
Main Objective Improve Soil Fertility
Benefits Free Soil Testing & Fertilizer Guidance
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website soilhealth.dac.gov.in

Benefits of Soil Health Card Scheme

  • Free soil testing for farmers
  • Improves soil fertility and crop production
  • Provides proper fertilizer recommendations
  • Reduces unnecessary fertilizer usage
  • Improves farmer income and productivity
  • Helps maintain soil health for long-term farming
  • Promotes balanced nutrient management

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian farmer
  • Farmers should own or cultivate agricultural land
  • Small and marginal farmers are eligible
  • Tenant farmers can also apply in some states
  • Applicants should provide land details

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Land Ownership Documents
  • Residence Certificate
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Identity Proof

Soil Testing Process

  1. Soil samples are collected from agricultural fields
  2. Samples are sent to authorized soil testing laboratories
  3. Laboratory checks soil nutrients and fertility levels
  4. Soil Health Card is prepared based on results
  5. Farmers receive fertilizer and nutrient recommendations

How to Apply Online for Soil Health Card Scheme?

  1. Visit the official Soil Health portal
  2. Open farmer registration section
  3. Fill personal and land details carefully
  4. Upload required documents
  5. Submit the application form online
  6. Soil sample collection process will begin
  7. After testing, Soil Health Card will be issued

Main Objectives of Soil Health Card Scheme

  • Promote balanced fertilizer use
  • Increase crop productivity
  • Improve soil quality and fertility
  • Reduce farming costs for farmers
  • Encourage scientific farming methods
