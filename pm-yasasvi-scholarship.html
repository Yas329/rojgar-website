PM YASASVI Scholarship 2026

PM YASASVI Scholarship Scheme is a government scholarship program launched to support meritorious students belonging to OBC, EBC and DNT categories across India.

The scheme provides financial assistance to eligible students studying in Class 9, Class 11 and higher education courses through scholarship benefits.

Benefits of PM YASASVI Scholarship

  • Financial support for education expenses
  • Scholarship assistance for deserving students
  • Encourages higher education among students
  • Supports economically weaker students
  • Online application and transparent selection process
  • Reduces educational financial burden
  • Promotes equal educational opportunities

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen
  • Student should belong to OBC, EBC or DNT category
  • Applicant must study in recognized institutions
  • Family income should meet eligibility limits
  • Students must meet academic performance criteria

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Caste Certificate
  • Income Certificate
  • Previous Class Marksheet
  • School ID Card
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Bank Passbook

How to Apply Online for PM YASASVI Scholarship?

  1. Visit the official PM YASASVI portal
  2. Complete student registration process
  3. Login using application credentials
  4. Fill personal and academic details carefully
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Verify application details properly
  7. Submit the application form online
