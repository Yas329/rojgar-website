E Shram Card Yojana 2026

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

E Shram Card Yojana is a welfare scheme launched by the Ministry of Labour and Employment for unorganized sector workers in India. Under this scheme, eligible workers receive a Universal Account Number (UAN) and can access government benefits, insurance coverage and financial assistance programs.

Official Website

E Shram Card Yojana Overview

Scheme Name E Shram Card Yojana
Launched By Ministry of Labour and Employment
Beneficiaries Unorganized Sector Workers
Registration Mode Online / CSC Center
Insurance Benefit ₹2 Lakh Accident Insurance
Official Website eshram.gov.in

Benefits of E Shram Card

  • ₹2 lakh accidental insurance coverage.
  • Government welfare scheme benefits.
  • Financial assistance during emergencies.
  • Universal Account Number (UAN) card.
  • Access to employment opportunities and social security schemes.

E Shram Card Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen.
  • Age should be between 16 to 59 years.
  • Applicant should work in unorganized sector.
  • Income taxpayer and EPFO/ESIC members may not be eligible.
  • Workers like labourers, street vendors, domestic workers and drivers can apply.

Documents Required for E Shram Registration

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Mobile Number linked with Aadhaar
  • Bank Account Details
  • IFSC Code
  • Passport Size Photograph

How to Apply Online for E Shram Card?

  1. Visit the official E Shram portal.
  2. Click on "Register on eShram".
  3. Enter Aadhaar linked mobile number.
  4. Verify OTP and Aadhaar details.
  5. Fill personal and occupation information.
  6. Enter bank account details carefully.
  7. Submit the registration form.
  8. Download E Shram Card PDF.

How to Check E Shram Card Balance?

Workers can check payment or balance updates through bank account statements, official government announcements or linked welfare scheme portals.

  • Check bank account linked with Aadhaar.
  • Visit CSC center for assistance.
  • Verify payment updates through official notifications.

What is UAN Number in E Shram Card?

UAN (Universal Account Number) is a unique 12-digit number generated after successful E Shram registration. This number helps workers access benefits and welfare schemes across India.

E Shram Card FAQs

What is E Shram Card?

E Shram Card is a government identity card for unorganized sector workers providing insurance and welfare scheme benefits.

Who can apply for E Shram Card?

Workers in unorganized sectors such as labourers, domestic workers, drivers and street vendors can apply.

What is the benefit of E Shram Card?

Workers receive accidental insurance coverage and access to welfare schemes.

How to download E Shram Card?

Applicants can download E Shram Card online after successful registration.
