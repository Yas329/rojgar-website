PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana 2026

Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana (PMKSY) is a government irrigation scheme launched to improve water use efficiency and provide irrigation facilities to farmers across India.

The main objective of this scheme is to ensure “Har Khet Ko Pani” by expanding irrigation coverage and promoting modern irrigation methods like drip irrigation and sprinkler irrigation.

PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana Overview

Scheme Name Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchai Yojana
Short Name PMKSY
Launched By Government of India
Beneficiaries Farmers
Main Objective Provide Irrigation Facilities
Application Mode Online / Offline
Official Website pmksy.gov.in

Benefits of PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana

  • Improves irrigation facilities for farmers
  • Promotes water-saving irrigation systems
  • Supports drip and sprinkler irrigation
  • Helps increase agricultural productivity
  • Reduces water wastage
  • Financial assistance for irrigation equipment
  • Improves crop production and farmer income

Subsidy Benefits Under PMKSY

Irrigation Type Subsidy Benefit
Drip Irrigation Up to 55% Subsidy
Sprinkler Irrigation Up to 45% Subsidy
Micro Irrigation Government Financial Assistance

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian farmer
  • Farmers should own agricultural land
  • Small and marginal farmers are eligible
  • Applicant should have valid land records
  • Farmers applying for irrigation systems can apply

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Land Ownership Documents
  • Bank Passbook
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Mobile Number
  • Residence Certificate
  • Identity Proof

How to Apply Online for PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana?

  1. Visit the official PMKSY website
  2. Open the farmer registration page
  3. Fill personal and agricultural details
  4. Select irrigation type and subsidy option
  5. Upload required documents
  6. Submit the application form online
  7. Verification process will be completed by authorities
  8. Approved subsidy amount will be provided

Main Objectives of PMKSY

  • Provide irrigation access to every farm
  • Increase water use efficiency
  • Encourage modern farming techniques
  • Promote sustainable agriculture
  • Improve crop productivity across India
