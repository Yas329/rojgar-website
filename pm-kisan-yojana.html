PM Kisan Yojana 2026

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a central government scheme launched to provide financial support to eligible farmers across India. Under this scheme, eligible farmers receive ₹6000 annually in three equal installments directly into their bank accounts.

Official Website

PM Kisan Yojana Overview

Scheme Name PM Kisan Samman Nidhi Yojana
Launched By Government of India
Department Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare
Benefit Amount ₹6000 Per Year
Payment Mode Direct Bank Transfer (DBT)
Beneficiaries Eligible Farmers
Official Website pmkisan.gov.in

PM Kisan Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicant must be an Indian citizen.
  • Only eligible farmers can apply.
  • Applicant should possess cultivable land records.
  • Bank account must be linked with Aadhaar.
  • Government employees and income taxpayers may not be eligible.

Documents Required for PM Kisan Registration

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Bank Passbook
  • Mobile Number
  • Land Records
  • Passport Size Photograph
  • Residence Certificate

How to Apply Online for PM Kisan Yojana?

  1. Visit the official PM Kisan portal.
  2. Click on "New Farmer Registration".
  3. Enter Aadhaar Number and captcha code.
  4. Fill all required personal details carefully.
  5. Upload required documents.
  6. Submit the application form.
  7. Save or print the application form for future use.

How to Check PM Kisan Beneficiary Status?

Farmers can check installment and beneficiary status online through the official PM Kisan portal using Aadhaar number, mobile number or registration number.

  1. Open PM Kisan official website.
  2. Click on "Beneficiary Status".
  3. Enter Aadhaar number or mobile number.
  4. Click on Get Data.
  5. Status details will appear on screen.

Benefits of PM Kisan Yojana

  • Direct financial support to farmers.
  • Helps farmers manage agricultural expenses.
  • Online registration and status checking facility.
  • Transparent DBT payment system.
  • Improves financial stability for small farmers.

PM Kisan Yojana FAQs

What is PM Kisan Yojana?

PM Kisan Yojana is a government scheme that provides ₹6000 yearly financial assistance to eligible farmers.

How many installments are provided?

The amount is provided in three installments of ₹2000 each.

Who can apply for PM Kisan Yojana?

Eligible Indian farmers with cultivable land records can apply.

How to check PM Kisan payment status?

Applicants can check status online through the official PM Kisan portal.
